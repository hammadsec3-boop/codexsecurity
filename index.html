How an engagement works

A structured, repeatable process from first contact to a measurably safer system.

  1. Discover

    Agree scope, goals and rules of engagement.

  2. Assess

    Map systems and the real attack surface.

  3. Identify

    Find and manually verify vulnerabilities.

  4. Secure

    Guide fixes with clear priority order.

  5. Report

    Deliver findings with evidence attached.

  6. Improve

    Retest fixes and plan the next cycle.

Frequently asked questions

What services do you provide?

Penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, cloud security, threat intelligence, security monitoring, and incident response.

Who needs cybersecurity testing?

Any organisation that stores customer data, runs online systems, or relies on digital tools — from startups to enterprises.

How long does an assessment take?

It depends on scope. Small applications can take days; larger environments take weeks. We confirm timing in the proposal.

Do you provide a written report?

Yes. Every engagement ends with a report covering findings, severity, evidence and clear remediation steps.

Can you help us fix what you find?

Yes. We give actionable guidance, support your developers, and retest to confirm the fixes hold.
