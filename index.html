Frequently asked questions

What is web application penetration testing?

Web application penetration testing is a simulated cyberattack on your website or app to identify security weaknesses before hackers can exploit them. It helps secure sensitive data and maintain user trust.

How often should I test my website for vulnerabilities?

We recommend testing at least once a year, or whenever you launch major updates. Regular testing ensures your application remains secure against evolving threats.

Do you offer custom security solutions?

Yes, Codex Security offers tailored security packages based on your business needs, size and risk level. Whether you’re a startup or an enterprise, we’ve got you covered.

How long does a penetration test take?

The duration depends on the complexity of your application. Most tests take between 3 and 7 days, and we’ll provide a full report with actionable insights.

Is my business too small to be a target for hackers?

Not at all. Small businesses are often targeted because they tend to have weaker security. Investing in cybersecurity is crucial no matter your size.

Inside a Codex finding

Proof, impact and the exact fix, written so your engineers can act the same day.

Critical
CXS-0417-F01CVSS 3.1 · 9.8Broken authentication

Password-reset tokens can be guessed, so any account can be taken over

The token is only a Base64-encoded user ID and timestamp, so an attacker can forge one for any victim.

Fix

Use a random 256-bit token, hash it at rest, expire it in 15 minutes and rate-limit the endpoint.
